PH Get Started · value demo, 2 versions

Proposal UI for the Android onboarding first screen; one version will be chosen for the Stage 1 test. The phone screens are 2× exports from Figma. Inside each version, C1 and C2 differ only in the message and call source. Figma source: 01_Onboarding · V1 / V2 × C1 / C2.

Drag the handle freely on either phone. Left alone for 2 s, it slides to After by itself, then returns to the middle.

Message: know who's calling on Phone, Viber & WhatsApp.Example call: a Viber call; the dialog shows “Viber” next to the country.
All phone pixels are 2× PNG exports of the Figma frames (720×1600, shown at 360×800), including the swipe handle with the Whoscall phone icon. V1 motion moves and fades cropped parts of those exports; the ringing halo and the red / green flood after a swipe are drawn by this page. Drag the handle left past the Decline threshold for “You avoided a high-risk call!”, or right to Answer for “Now you know who’s calling you!”; a short drag springs back. Each phone runs on its own. In V2 only the slider line, handle and Before/After labels are drawn by this page. Numbers and labels are examples, not a real lookup.
